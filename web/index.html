      About the data

      This map shows paving and road surface work that Pima County and the City of Tucson have published in their public map layers. Click a road to see every job on record for that block.

      An empty history does not mean a road was never repaired. It means neither agency has a record of it in these layers. County records start in fiscal 2017. City records go back to about 2011 for some major projects. State highways and the other towns are shown but their records are not loaded.

      Cost is not shown yet. Neither agency publishes dollars per road, so cost appears only once a job has been linked to a contract document.
